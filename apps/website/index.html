Every screen. In harmony.

Free church presentation software for lyrics, Bible and slides

Worship Q is a free, open-source church presentation app for macOS and Windows that shows song lyrics, Bible passages, slides and videos on the projector and the live stream. Plan each week's run sheet and present it, with no account and no internet needed.

Opening it the first time

Mac
Drag Worship Q to Applications and open it.
Windows
Worship Q is not yet signed with Microsoft, so Windows asks before opening it once. If “Windows protected your PC” appears, choose More info, then Run anyway. Updates after that need nothing.

What it does

Songs in any language

Search by title or lyrics, and Chinese songs by pinyin or initials. A line in one language and the same line in another share a slide.

Bible passages

With your church's server: type a reference such as Mark 15:5-6, or browse by book and chapter, and show up to two versions side by side.

A run sheet for every meeting

Plan on a calendar, reuse an earlier week, and open on today's service, already live.

Slides, PDFs, photos and videos

With your church's server, PowerPoint, Keynote and PDF decks become slides beside the songs. Videos download ahead of time and never stream during the service.

Projector and live stream together

A full-screen slideshow with image or video backgrounds, and a subtitle overlay for OBS. Switch OBS scenes and start the stream from Worship Q.

Control from a phone

Scan a QR code, approve the phone once, and move through the slides or add a Bible passage from anywhere in the room.

Several computers, one presentation

Every connected computer shows the same slide. If the presenting one fails, another carries on from the same slide.

Live audio

Send the sound from the mixing desk to other computers and phones, so someone outside the room can switch slides in time.

Works on its own

No account or internet needed. Connect to your church's own server for shared songs, media, Bible passages and phones.

Free and open source

MIT licence, no ads, no subscription. Installed copies update themselves, never while the slideshow is open.
