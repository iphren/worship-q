Opening it the first time

Worship Q is not yet signed with Apple or Microsoft, so your computer asks before opening it once. Updates after that need nothing.

Mac
Drag Worship Q to Applications and open it. When macOS says it cannot verify the app, choose Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and choose Open Anyway.
Windows
If “Windows protected your PC” appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Songs in any language

Search by title or lyrics, in Chinese, pinyin or initials. Two-language lyrics share a slide.

A run sheet for every meeting

Plan on a calendar, reuse an earlier week, and open on today's service, already live.

Projector and stream together

Works on its own
