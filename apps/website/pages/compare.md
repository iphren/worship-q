# Worship Q compared

Looking for a free alternative to ProPresenter or EasyWorship, or weighing Worship Q against
OpenLP, FreeShow or Proclaim? Here is how they compare on the facts each one publishes.

|               | Price                         | Runs on                                                    | Open source      |
| ------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| **Worship Q** | Free, no paid tier            | macOS 11 or later, Windows 10 or later                     | Yes, MIT licence |
| ProPresenter  | Paid subscription (new users) | macOS, Windows                                             | No               |
| EasyWorship   | Paid subscription             | macOS, Windows                                             | No               |
| Proclaim      | Paid subscription             | macOS, Windows; browser, iOS and Android with Proclaim Pro | No               |
| OpenLP        | Free                          | Windows, macOS, Linux                                      | Yes, GPL         |
| FreeShow      | Free                          | Windows, macOS, Linux                                      | Yes, GPL 3.0     |

Checked on 17 November 2026 against each product's own website and support pages. Prices and
features change; check with each vendor before you choose.

## Where Worship Q is different

- **Free, with nothing held back.** No subscription, no paid tier, no account. Phones, Bible
  passages and slides work through a free public server that keeps nothing. The church server
  that adds sharing between computers is free too, and it runs on your own machine, so your
  songs stay with your church.
- **Two or more languages on one slide.** Each line of a song and its translations share a
  slide, and Chinese songs can be found by pinyin or initials, in either script.
  [More](/multilingual-lyrics/)
- **Bible passages in two versions side by side,** kept in step verse by verse, found by
  typing the reference. [More](/bible/)
- **Made for the live stream too:** a subtitle overlay for OBS, and OBS's scenes, streaming
  and recording controlled from Worship Q or a phone. [More](/obs/)
- **Several computers, one presentation,** with another computer carrying on from the same
  slide if the presenting one fails, and the venue's Wi-Fi keeping them together without
  internet. [More](/several-computers/)
- **Phones as remotes** with no app to install: scan a QR code and approve the phone once.
  [More](/phone-remote/)

## Where others are ahead

- **No Linux version.** Worship Q runs on macOS and Windows; OpenLP and FreeShow also run on
  Linux.
- **No import from other software yet.** Songs from ProPresenter, EasyWorship or OpenLP are
  pasted in as text. If your church needs an import to move over, email
  [hello@ihs.technology](mailto:hello@ihs.technology).
- **A young project.** The established products have years of features, such as stage
  displays and extensive media libraries, and large communities.
- **The Windows app is not yet signed** with Microsoft, so Windows asks once before opening
  it.
