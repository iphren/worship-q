# Control the slides from a phone

Anyone you approve can run the slides from their phone, from anywhere in the room: the sound
desk, the front row, or the stage.

## Connecting a phone

Open the phone icon at the bottom of Worship Q's sidebar and scan the QR code. The phone asks
for control and shows a four-digit code; approve it on the computer when the codes match. No
app to install: the remote is a web page.

The approval belongs to the workspace, so the phone keeps working with every computer of the
church's workspace, until someone chooses **Disconnect** or **Disconnect all**.

## What a phone can do

- Move between slides, jump to any slide of the run sheet, and blank the screen.
- With **Edit**: search the library, add, remove or reorder songs on the live run sheet, add
  a Bible passage, or add a website by typing its address. Every operator sees each change.
- Play, pause and move through a video while it is on screen.
- Switch OBS scenes and start or stop the stream and recording.
- Listen to the sound from the mixing desk, to switch slides in time from outside the room.

A phone cannot edit songs, go live with another date, or change settings.

## What it needs

Phones connect through your church's free Worship Q server, so the phone needs the internet
at the venue. [Setting up the server](/server/#remote-control).
