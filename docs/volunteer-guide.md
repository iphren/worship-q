# Worship Q — Volunteer Guide

Everything stays on this computer. You need no account and no internet. If your church runs a
Worship Q server, several computers and phones can share one presentation; see **Several
computers** below.

## 1. Add a song

1. Open **Songs** and choose **New song**.
2. Type the title, then type or paste the lyrics, one line of lyrics per line.
3. Leave a blank line between sections (verse, chorus…).
4. The **Slides** column shows how the song will be split. By default every two lines make
   one slide, so a line in one language followed by the same line in another appear together.
5. Choose **Save**.

## 2. Build a run sheet

1. Open **Calendar**. It opens on your next meeting day.
2. Search for a song under the run sheet and click it to add it. Use ↑ ↓ ✕ to reorder or remove.
3. To reuse an earlier week, click that date and choose **Copy to …**.

Other dates are read-only until you choose **Edit run sheet** on them.

## 3. Present

1. Connect the projector or TV as a second screen.
2. Open **Present**. Under **Slideshow screen**, pick the projector and choose **Open
   slideshow**. Worship Q remembers it for next week. To move the slideshow to another
   screen, pick that screen, then close the slideshow and open it again. Or choose
   **Windowed**, drag the slideshow to the screen you want and choose **Full screen**.
3. The app opens on **Present**, already live: with today's run sheet if it has songs,
   otherwise with the next meeting day. To present another date, choose **Go live** on it
   in the calendar.
4. Click any slide to show it. ↓ and ↑ step through the slides of the current song, and →
   and ← move to the next or previous song. Backspace hides the lyrics and keeps the
   background; ↓, ↑ or Space then shows the same slide again. Delete (fn + delete on a
   Mac laptop) clears the screen and stays live; ↓ or Space then starts from the first
   slide. The same keys work when the slideshow window is the one in front.

## 4. Fix a lyric during the service

Choose **Edit lyrics** above the song, correct the text and **Save**. The screen updates
straight away and stays on the same slide.

## 5. When you finish

Just close the app, or choose **Clear screen** to take the lyrics down. The run sheet open
for planning is then always today's when today is a meeting day, otherwise the next
meeting day's. Everything is saved as you go; if the laptop is closed or restarts
mid-service, reopening Worship Q carries on where it was.

## Settings

**Settings** holds your meeting days, lines per slide, the slideshow font, text size and
background (a color, an image or a looping video), and **Backup**, which saves the whole
workspace to one file you can keep somewhere safe.

## Several computers

When the computers are connected to your church's server (**Settings → Church connection**),
they share one presentation. The first to open holds it; the others follow and show the same
slide on their own screens. Anyone can click a slide, go live with another date or edit the
live run sheet from any of them; other operators see edits in a banner.

- **Use this computer** (Settings → Church connection) makes this computer hold the
  presentation. Nothing changes on any screen.
- If the presenting computer fails, the others show **The presenting computer is not
  responding**. Choose **Carry on here** on one of them: it continues from the same slide.
  When the first computer comes back, it follows.
- **Viewer mode** (Settings) is for a projector computer that should only follow: it shows
  the presentation and never starts or holds one, and reopens as a viewer. With nothing
  live it shows **Waiting for presentation**.
- Without internet at the venue, computers on the same Wi-Fi keep presenting together.
  Settings says how each is connected: **On this network** or **Through the church server**.

## Phones

Open the phone icon at the bottom of the sidebar on any computer. A phone that scans the QR
code asks for control and shows a four-digit code; approve it when the code matches. The
phone then works with every computer of the workspace until someone chooses **Disconnect**
or **Disconnect all**.

## Browser outputs and connected devices

**Settings → Browser outputs** makes a link that shows the slideshow or the subtitle overlay
in any browser, or in OBS on a computer without Worship Q. Copy the link when it is made; it
is shown only once. **Revoke** a link to stop it. On this computer, OBS can show the
slideshow without internet at the address shown there.

**Settings → Connected devices** lists everything following the presentation, with
**Disconnect** for phones and browser outputs.
