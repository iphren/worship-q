# Worship Q — Volunteer Guide

Everything stays on this computer. You need no account and no internet. If your church runs a
Worship Q server, several computers and phones can share one presentation; see **Several
computers** below.

## 1. Add a song

1. Open **Library** and choose **New song**.
2. Type the title, then type or paste the lyrics, one line of lyrics per line.
3. Leave a blank line between sections (verse, chorus…).
4. The **Slides** column shows how the song will be split. By default every two lines make
   one slide, so a line in one language followed by the same line in another appear together.
5. Choose **Save**.

## 2. Build a run sheet

1. Open **Calendar**. It opens on your next meeting day.
2. Search for a song under the run sheet and click it to add it. Use ↑ ↓ ✕ to reorder or remove.
3. To reuse an earlier week, click that date and choose **Copy to …**.

Other dates are read-only until you choose **Edit run sheet** on them.

## 3. Present

1. Connect the projector or TV as a second screen.
2. Open **Present**. Under **Slideshow screen**, pick the projector and choose **Open
   slideshow**. Worship Q remembers it for next week. To move the slideshow to another
   screen, pick that screen, then close the slideshow and open it again. Or choose
   **Windowed**, drag the slideshow to the screen you want and choose **Full screen**.
3. The app opens on **Present**, already live: with today's run sheet if it has songs,
   otherwise with the next meeting day. To present another date, choose **Go live** on it
   in the calendar.
4. Click any slide to show it. ↓ and ↑ step through the slides of the current song, and →
   and ← move to the next or previous song: its title is highlighted in indigo, so you can see
   which song the keys act on before you show it. Enter moves on through the whole run sheet:
   the next slide, and after the last slide of a song, the first slide of the next one.
   Space hides the lyrics and keeps the background; press Space again to show the same
   slide. (Space does not move to the next slide, as it does in PowerPoint or Keynote.)
   Delete (fn + delete on a Mac laptop) clears the screen and stays live; Enter, ↓ or
   Space then starts from the first slide. The same keys work when the slideshow window is
   the one in front.

## 4. Fix a lyric during the service

Choose **Edit lyrics** above the song, correct the text or title and **Save**. The screen updates
straight away and stays on the same slide.

Slide decks, PDFs, photos and websites show **Edit title** instead, which renames the item;
a video's also has **Autoplay**.

## 5. When you finish

Just close the app, or choose **Clear screen** to take the lyrics down. The run sheet open
for planning is then always today's when today is a meeting day, otherwise the next
meeting day's. Everything is saved as you go; if the laptop is closed or restarts
mid-service, reopening Worship Q carries on where it was.

## Slides, PDFs, photos and videos

These need your church's Worship Q server (see **Several computers**).

- **Add them** from **Library → Media → Add media…**, with **Media…** next to the song search
  of a run sheet (added at the end), or by dragging files from Finder or Explorer onto a run
  sheet: a blue line shows where they will land. Several files at once are fine.
- **Two bars.** First a bar fills as the file is sent, with its percentage. Slides, PDFs and
  photos then show moving stripes while the church server works on them. Videos are ready as
  soon as they are sent. In a run sheet the bars are on the item itself; in the library they
  are under the search box. You can move to another page meanwhile. ✕ cancels while
  sending; if something goes wrong, the reason shows with **Try again**.
- **Too big?** The message says how big the file is and what the server accepts. Export a
  smaller version (for a video, MP4 with H.264 plays everywhere) and add it again.
- **iPhone photos (HEIC).** A Mac turns them into JPEG on its own. On Windows, export the
  photo as JPEG first, or set the iPhone to **Settings → Camera → Formats → Most
  Compatible**.
- **The ⋯ menu** on each run sheet item has **Edit…** (lyrics, a title, or a video's title
  and **Autoplay**), **Download original**, **Upload new version…** and **Remove**. A new
  version keeps the item's title and its place in every run sheet; if it fails, nothing
  changes.

### Videos

Every computer downloads the videos of today's and upcoming run sheets ahead of time; a
violet bar on the item shows it, and nothing streams during the service. A video item starts
paused, unless **Autoplay** is on in its Edit window. While a video is live, a control bar
above the slides plays, pauses and moves through it, and phones get the same bar. The keys
change too: Space plays or pauses and ↑ and ↓ jump 10 seconds back and forward. ← and →
move to another item as usual, and Enter presents the next item. Leave a video for a reading and come back: it carries on where it was. Blanking
pauses it; showing the screen again carries on. **End** forgets where videos were.

Video sound plays from the slideshow window. Choose where under **Settings → Audio on
this computer**; the app asks once to use the microphone, which it only uses to list your
devices. Browser slideshow pages and OBS play the sound too. A browser may wait until the page
is clicked: the video then plays silently with **Click for sound**.

## Bible passages

When the speaker reads a passage that is not in the run sheet, click the **book** button
beside the song search on the Present page (or in the calendar's run sheet editor).

- **Type it** the way you would write it: `Mark 15:5-6`, `Mk 15.5-6`, `马可福音15章5-6节`,
  `可15:5`. Or **browse**: choose a book, then a chapter, then the first and the last verse
  (choose one verse twice for that verse alone). **Continue into chapter …** carries a range
  into the next chapter. Words that are not a reference, such as `love your enemies`, search
  the Bible after a pause.
- The **preview** shows the text in the workspace's versions side by side. Check it, then:
  - **Present ▶** (or Enter) adds the passage right after the item on screen and shows it at
    once. With nothing on screen it goes at the end of the run sheet.
  - **Add to run sheet** puts it in the same place without showing it, for a passage the
    speaker will read later. In the calendar, only Add is offered.
- Typing a reference into the song search, such as `Mark 15:5`, offers **Open Mark 15:5 in the
  Bible** above the songs.
- **Versions** are the chips beside the search box: **✕** removes one, **◀** shows it first,
  and **+** adds one of those the church offers, at most two. They belong to the workspace, so
  every computer gets the same; they are also in **Settings → Bible versions**.
- A passage is a **run sheet item** with a teal book badge. Move or remove it like any item;
  **Edit…** opens the Bible window on it to change the verses or its versions, keeping its
  place. Changing the workspace's versions never changes passages already added.
- **Notes under a passage** (ⓘ) explain why it may look different from the speaker's Bible:
  a version that joins two verses into one (当代译本 joins 1 Chronicles 16:12–13), or one that
  has no such verse (NIVUK has no Revelation 12:18). The screen never shows these notes.
- Passages have **their own background**: **Settings → Background → Bible** sets one for every
  passage, apart from songs' backgrounds. They are on black until you choose another, or
  choose **Slideshow background** to use the workspace's.
- Passages are **not shown on the subtitle overlay**. To show one on the stream, switch OBS to
  a scene with the slides.
- New passages need the church server; passages already in a run sheet present offline.
- On a **phone**, **Edit → Add a Bible passage** does the same, except editing a passage
  already added. A reference typed into the phone's library search offers **Open … in the
  Bible** too.

## Settings

**Settings** holds your meeting days, lines per slide, the slideshow font, text size and
background (a color, an image or a looping video), and **Backup**, which saves the whole
workspace to one file you can keep somewhere safe.

## Several computers

When the computers are connected to your church's server (**Settings → Church connection**),
they share one presentation. The first to open holds it; the others follow and show the same
slide on their own screens. Anyone can click a slide, go live with another date or edit the
live run sheet from any of them; other operators see edits in a banner.

- **Use this computer** (Settings → Church connection) makes this computer hold the
  presentation. Nothing changes on any screen.
- If the presenting computer fails, the others show **The presenting computer is not
  responding**. Choose **Carry on here** on one of them: it continues from the same slide.
  When the first computer comes back, it follows.
- **Viewer mode** (Settings) is for a projector computer that should only follow: it shows
  the presentation and never starts or holds one, and reopens as a viewer. With nothing
  live it shows **Waiting for presentation**.
- Without internet at the venue, computers on the same Wi-Fi keep presenting together.
  Settings says how each is connected: **On this network** or **Through the church server**.

To connect a new computer, enter the server address and a name for the computer under
**Settings → Church connection** and choose **Next**. The computer shows a code; one of your
church's administrators approves it from their browser, and the computer connects. If your
church gave you a connection code instead, choose **I have a connection code instead**.

## Church songs

When your church shares a library, songs that come from it carry a **Church** badge. You can
present them as they are. If you edit one, your changes become your workspace's own version:
the church's version stays as it is for everyone else, and the song now shows **2 versions**. A
song's background is different: it is your workspace's choice, so changing it never makes a
version, and the church's later changes to the song still reach you.

Open a song with two versions and you see a switch at the top: **Original version** (the
church's) and **Our version**. The original version is read-only; ours can be edited, and
**Delete our version** beside the switch drops it for good and goes back to the church's song.
Choose the one your workspace presents and press **Save**: like any edit, the choice takes
effect only when saved. It is for this song in this workspace only, and every computer of your
workspace follows it. If the church deletes its version, the song
shows only yours until the church brings its version back.

Removing a church song hides it in your workspace only; the church and other workspaces keep
it.

## Phones

Open the phone icon at the bottom of the sidebar on any computer. A phone that scans the QR
code asks for control and shows a four-digit code; approve it when the code matches. The
phone then works with every computer of the workspace until someone chooses **Disconnect**
or **Disconnect all**.

## OBS

Worship Q can switch scenes, stream and record in OBS for you, from any computer of the
workspace and from phones.

**Once, on the computer OBS runs on.** In OBS, open **Tools → WebSocket Server Settings**,
tick **Enable WebSocket server** and click **Apply**. Then in Worship Q, open **Settings →
OBS** and turn on **Control OBS on this computer**. Worship Q reads OBS's password itself when
it can; if it says a password is needed, click **Show Connect Info** in OBS and copy the
password into Settings. Choose the two scenes of the **Switch button**, for example your
lyrics scene and your camera scene. This computer can be a viewer.

**During the service.** On the Present page, while OBS is connected, a small picture of what
is on the stream sits above the slides, with the switch button under it and whether
**Stream**, **Record** and **Camera** (OBS's virtual camera) are on beside it. The **OBS** tab
beside **Run sheet** and **Slideshow** has every scene and starts and stops **Stream**,
**Record** and **Camera**. Phones have the same in a row under the run sheet title. The dot shows the connection: green connected, amber connecting,
grey OBS not running, red refused. Stopping the stream or a recording asks first.

OBS and Worship Q can be opened in either order. If another computer turns on its own OBS
link, it takes over and this one turns off; turn it on again to take it back. Turning the link
off removes the OBS controls everywhere.

## Live audio

One computer can send the sound from the mixing desk to the other computers, phones and the
church's administrators, so someone outside the room can switch slides in time.

**Once, on the computer with the mixer feed.** Connect the desk to the computer through an
audio interface or a USB input, not the computer's own microphone, which picks up the room.
In **Settings → Audio on this computer**, choose it as the **Audio input** and turn on
**Broadcast live audio**. Watch the **Input level** move when the desk sends sound. That is
all: from then on the computer broadcasts by itself whenever Worship Q opens, even if the
interface is plugged in later. Turn off any screen lock on this computer; Worship Q keeps the
display awake while broadcasting.

**What the status means.** **Live** with the number listening; **Waiting for input** means
the chosen interface is not plugged in, and the sound comes back by itself when it is;
**The microphone was refused** means the system's privacy settings must allow Worship Q;
**Bring this window into view** means the window is minimised or covered and the system will
not start the microphone until it is seen.

**Listening.** On any other computer, the Present page shows **Listen** with a headphones
button; the button becomes a stop icon while listening. Nothing plays until you press it, and
it is off again after a restart. It plays on the **Listen plays on** device chosen in
Settings, such as your headphones, which you can change while listening. Phones have the same
button under the run sheet title. The figure beside it is the network and buffer delay, and
**Direct** or **Relayed**. Up to ten can listen at once.

**On a phone,** the sound may stop when the screen locks or the browser goes to the
background. Keep the screen on and the browser in front while listening.

If another computer turns on **Broadcast live audio**, it takes over and this one turns off;
turn it on again to take it back.



**Settings → Browser outputs** makes a link that shows the slideshow or the subtitle overlay
in any browser, or in OBS on a computer without Worship Q. Copy the link when it is made; it
is shown only once. **Revoke** a link to stop it. On this computer, OBS can show the
slideshow without internet at the address shown there.

**Settings → Connected devices** lists everything following the presentation, with
**Disconnect** for phones and browser outputs.
